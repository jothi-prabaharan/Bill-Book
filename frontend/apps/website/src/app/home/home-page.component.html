Integrated SaaS Cloud

Everything Needed To Run Your Enterprise

Eliminate disconnected software. Bill-Book consolidates your counter billing, supply chain, accounts, human resources, and customer support into a single reliable system.

GST Invoicing & E-Way Bills

Generate B2B and B2C invoices with auto-tax split (CGST, SGST, IGST), HSN/SAC lookups, e-Invoicing QR codes, and 1-click GSTR-1 JSON exports.

Multi-Warehouse Inventory

Track items across central godowns and branch counters. Supports serial numbers, batch tracking, expiry date monitoring, and automated stock adjustments.

High-Speed POS Counter

Sub-second keyboard shortcuts, barcode reader integration, cash drawer kick, electronic scale reading, and rapid slip thermal printing.

Bank Reconciliation

Direct statement imports, spend and receive money mapping, inter-bank transfers, and automatic match recognition for uncleared cheques.

Indian Accounting Ledger

Standard Indian Chart of Accounts with double-entry general ledger, manual journal entries, trial balance, P&L statements, and balance sheets.

CRM & Lead Pipeline

Captures website inquiries, assigns leads, tracks demo conversion, schedules customer follow-ups, and resolves SLA helpdesk tickets.
