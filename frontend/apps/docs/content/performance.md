# Performance Appraisals and Reviews

Performance (TK-58, H11) covers the complete employee appraisal and evaluation lifecycle: review cycles, eligibility criteria, rating scales, competencies, goals, multi-level evaluation workflows, calibration sessions, final release, employee acknowledgement, and automated salary revision integration with Payroll.

## Core Features

- **Rating Scales & Competencies**: Configurable rating scales with descriptive bands, definitions, and weightages. Centralized competency library organized into behavioral, functional, and leadership groups.
- **Review Cycles & Eligibility**: Define annual, half-yearly, quarterly, or probation cycles with timeline bounds (self-review end date, manager review end date). Automated eligibility calculation based on minimum tenure, departments, and employee status.
- **Goal Management**: SMART goal tracking with target dates, metrics, weightages, and progress statuses (NotStarted, InProgress, Completed, Deferred).
- **Multi-Level Evaluation & Approval Workflow Routing**: Structured employee self-evaluations with evidence notes, followed by hierarchical multi-level manager reviews and approval workflow integration. Reviewers cannot alter employee self-evaluations, preserving an audit trail of independent assessments.
- **Calibration & Bell-Curve Adjustments**: Committee-level calibration sessions to review rating distributions across teams, apply moderated adjustments with reason codes, and lock final ratings.
- **Release, Acknowledgement & Payroll Revisions**: Published appraisal results allow employee acknowledgement with optional comments. High-performing ratings can automatically trigger salary revisions in Payroll with effective dates and revision histories.
