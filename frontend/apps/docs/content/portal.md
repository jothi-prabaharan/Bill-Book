# Customer portal

A customer can see their own account on the portal without a staff login. They open it from a
portal link you send them. See [Portal access](masters#portal-access) for making and revoking links.

Everything on the portal is the customer's own and nobody else's. A document that belongs to
another customer, or that is still a draft, answers exactly as one that does not exist.

## Your account

The first page a customer sees.

| Figure | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Outstanding** | What they owe, less any advance they have paid and not yet used |
| **Overdue** | The part of that owed on invoices past their due date |
| **Traded this year** | Posted invoices less posted credit notes since the branch's financial year began |
| **Traded in all** | The same, for all time |

Below the figures are the five most recent invoices and credit notes. An invoice opens its own page.

## Invoices

Every invoice that was posted, newest first, including any since voided. Drafts never appear.
Each card shows the date, the due date, the total and a status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Nothing received yet, and not yet due |
| **Part-paid** | Some received, and not yet due |
| **Overdue** | Money still owed past the due date |
| **Paid** | Nothing left to pay |
| **Void** | Withdrawn; nothing is owed on it |

An invoice's page shows its lines, the totals, what is still owed, and **Download PDF**. The PDF is
the same file that was filed when the invoice was posted, so the customer gets the copy your staff
see.

## Statement

The account's history with a running balance.

- **What you owe** lists invoices, receipts and credit notes against the customer's receivable.
- **What we owe you** appears only for a contact who is also one of your vendors. It lists bills and
  payments, and its balance is what you owe them.

Each line shows the document's own number, and an invoice opens in the portal.
