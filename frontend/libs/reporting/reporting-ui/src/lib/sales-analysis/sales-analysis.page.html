Sales are posted invoices and POS sales, by invoice date, less posted credit notes. Net sales are before tax and after discount; gross and discount are as keyed, so a price that includes tax shows it in gross. Quantity is in each item's base unit, and a credit note takes units off only when it is a sales return. Cost is what the stock movements the sales caused cost once costing has settled them. A line whose cost is not settled yet is counted in the net sales, its cost is left out and marked pending, and the group has no margin until it is, so the margin is never overstated. A line with no item is one group of its own. The total is of everything the filters match, not only the groups shown.
