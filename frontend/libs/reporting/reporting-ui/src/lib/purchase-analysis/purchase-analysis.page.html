Purchases are posted bills, by bill date, less posted debit notes. Net purchases are before tax and after discount; gross and discount are as keyed, so a price that includes tax shows it in gross. Quantity is in each item's base unit, and a debit note takes units off only when it is a purchase return; a price correction or a discount takes value off and no units. The average cost is net purchases over units, and is shown only when adding up by item, where a unit means one thing. A fixed asset bought on a bill is not stock, so it is left out unless you include it. A line with no item, such as freight or a service, is one group of its own. The total is of everything the filters match, not only the groups shown.
