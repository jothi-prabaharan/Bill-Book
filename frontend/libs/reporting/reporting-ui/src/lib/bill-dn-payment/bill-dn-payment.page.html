A payment is listed by its payment date; a draft was never paid, a voided payment was taken back and a TDS challan pays the government rather than a vendor, so none of those is listed. The documents under a payment are the bills its lines settled, with the vendor's own bill number. A payment can settle more than left the bank, because the lines also settle any TDS withheld from the vendor, and anything it did not settle, such as an advance, is shown on its own line. A payment in another currency shows its amounts in that currency, with the amount in {{ report.baseCurrencyCode }} beneath. The total paid is of every payment the filters match, not only those shown.
