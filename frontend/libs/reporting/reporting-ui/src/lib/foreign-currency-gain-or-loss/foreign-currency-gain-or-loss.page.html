A balance is what the books hold in the currency: money owed to the branch is positive and money it owes is negative. At booked rates is that balance in {{ report.baseCurrencyCode }} at the rates the documents were booked at; at the revaluation rate it is the balance times the rate above. Unrealized is the second less the first, so a rising rate is a gain on a receivable and a loss on a payable. Nothing is posted for it. Realized is what settlements in the period posted to Realized FX Gain/Loss, and its total is that account's movement on the Profit and Loss for the same dates. A posting with no foreign-currency balance, such as a manual journal, belongs to no currency and is shown on its own line.
