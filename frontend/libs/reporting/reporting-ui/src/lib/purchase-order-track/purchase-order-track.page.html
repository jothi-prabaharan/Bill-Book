An order is listed by its order date. Received is the lines' own running figure, advanced by each posted goods receipt against the order, and counts only what was accepted. Billed is added up from the posted bills that name the order's lines, directly or through a goods receipt; a draft or voided bill bills nothing. To receive is ordered less received and to bill is ordered less billed, on every line. An order is received when every line is, and billed when every line is billed in full; closed short means it was closed with a line not received. Values are before tax at the order's price per unit, so a price the vendor changed on the bill shows in the bills raised, not here. A voided or cancelled order is listed only under Cancelled. The total at the foot is of every order the filters match, not only those shown.
