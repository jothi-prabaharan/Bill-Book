A challan is listed by its challan date. Invoiced is each line's own running figure, advanced by an invoice raised from the challan; to bill is delivered less invoiced, on every line. A challan is invoiced when every line is billed in full, not invoiced when nothing is, and partly invoiced between. An invoice raised from the sales order that does not name the challan bills the order's lines and does not advance the challan's own figure, so the challan still reads as unbilled. Only a sale challan is a supply; job work, approval, branch transfer and sample challans move goods without a sale, so goods on them are not to bill unless the customer keeps them and an invoice names the challan. A void challan was withdrawn as a draft and delivered nothing, so it is listed only under Void. The total at the foot is of every challan the filters match, not only those shown.
