Opening is the stock at the start of the From date, every movement before it, and closing is the stock at the end of the To date. Purchased is goods received less goods sent back to vendors, valued at what they cost; sold is goods issued to customers less goods they returned, with its value the net sales before tax and COGS what that stock cost; adjusted is every other movement, counts, opening entries and transfers, signed. Opening plus purchased less sold plus adjusted is closing, in quantity and in value. Profit is sales less COGS, and is blank while a movement of what was sold has not been costed yet; a "Cost pending" item has such a movement and its values are short by its cost. Stock is one pool for the branch, so nothing is split by warehouse, and the total at the foot is of values only, since quantities in different units do not add, over every item the filters match and not only those shown.
