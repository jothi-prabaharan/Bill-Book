An open document is one with a balance left at the To date. A settlement is taken off the document it settles at the rate that document was booked at, so the document shows what is still owed: the invoice of USD 1,000 less the receipts that settled it. A receipt that settles nothing in particular, an advance, is a document of its own with a negative balance. Documents that net to nothing are not listed. On an open document the rate column reads the rate it was booked at and then the revaluation rate; the small figure under its base amount is the amount at that rate, and Unrealized is the difference, a gain on a receivable when the rate rises and a loss on a payable. A settlement shows its Realized gain or loss on the date it was posted: the rate the document was booked at, then the rate the money moved at, and the amount settled. Each account's rows can differ by a paisa or two from its total, which is rounded once; the account and currency totals are those of the Foreign Currency Gain or Loss report. A posting with no foreign balance, such as a manual journal, belongs to no currency and is listed on its own.
