A receipt is listed by its receipt date; a draft is not a collection and a voided receipt was taken back, so neither is listed. The documents under a receipt are what its lines settled. A receipt can settle more than reached the bank, because the lines also settle any TDS the customer withheld, and anything it did not settle, such as an advance, is shown on its own line. A receipt in another currency shows its amounts in that currency, with the amount in {{ report.baseCurrencyCode }} beneath. The total collected is of every receipt the filters match, not only those shown.
