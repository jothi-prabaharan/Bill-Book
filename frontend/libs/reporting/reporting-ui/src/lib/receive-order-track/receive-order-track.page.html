A receipt is listed by its receipt date, and only a posted one: a draft has received nothing. Value is what the accepted goods are worth, the line's value before tax over what was delivered times what was accepted, which is what the receipt debits to Inventory; goods that were rejected at the door are not in it. Billed is the share of it that posted bills naming the receipt's lines add up to, at the receipt's own cost, and to bill is value less billed; a draft or voided bill bills nothing. A receipt is billed when every line with goods accepted is billed in full, partly billed when something is billed but not every line in full, and nothing to bill when every line was rejected. The total at the foot is of every receipt the filters match, not only those shown.
