A receipt is listed by its receipt date, and only a posted one: a draft has received nothing. Delivered is what the vendor brought, rejected what was refused at the door and accepted what went into stock, which is the quantity of the stock-in movement the receipt made. A unit costs the line's value before tax over what was delivered, so rejecting goods does not raise the cost of the rest; the value is that cost times what was accepted, which is what the receipt debits to Inventory. Billed is what posted bills naming the line add up to. Choosing an item or warehouse lists only receipts with such a line, and only those lines. The total at the foot is in {{ report.baseCurrencyCode }} and is of every receipt the filters match, not only those shown.
