A sheet posts as one document, however many items are on it — so the accounts show one adjustment for a count, not one per line. A draft moves nothing and holds no number; both arrive together when it posts. A posted sheet is never edited or deleted, because its stock has already moved: correct it by reversing, which writes the mirror of it.
